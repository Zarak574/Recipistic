## Overall Description of Recipistic

 **Recipistic** is a recipe discovery and cooking website designed to make finding and preparing meals easier. The main purpose of the website is to provide users with a convenient platform where they can **search, explore, save, and discover recipes from different cuisines around the world**.

 The website allows users to search for specific dishes such as burgers, pizzas, sandwiches, desserts, and more. Recipes can also be explored according to **meal categories**, including breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and teatime.

 A key feature of Recipistic is its **health and dietary preference system**. Users can browse recipes based on dietary requirements such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, keto-friendly, paleo, low-sugar, and other preferences. This helps users find meals that fit their individual lifestyles and nutritional needs.

 Registered users can create an account and access additional features such as a **personal dashboard, profile, and saved recipes**. The save functionality allows users to bookmark recipes they are interested in and easily access them later.

 The website also includes a **recipe detail system**, where users can select a recipe to view more information about it, as well as a search feature that makes finding specific recipes easier.

 ### Main Features

 - **Recipe discovery:** Browse recipes from different cuisines and food categories.
- **Recipe search:** Search for dishes using keywords such as pizza, burger, pasta, sandwich, etc.
- **Meal categories:** Explore recipes for breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and teatime.
- **Dietary filters:** Find recipes based on health and dietary preferences such as vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, keto, dairy-free, and more.
- **Recipe saving:** Registered users can save their favorite recipes for later.
- **User accounts:** Users can log in and access their profile, dashboard, and saved recipes.
- **Recipe details:** View individual recipes and their relevant cooking information.
- **Responsive design:** The website is designed to work across different screen sizes and devices.
- **Light/Dark mode:** Users can switch between light and dark themes.
- **Contact system:** Users can send messages or feedback through the contact form.
- **External recipe data:** The project is designed to use the **Edamam Recipe API** for recipe-related data.

 ### Purpose of the Project

 The overall goal of Recipistic is to **simplify the cooking experience**. Instead of searching through multiple websites to find suitable recipes, users can use one platform to discover meals, filter them according to their preferences, save recipes they like, and get inspiration for what to cook.
